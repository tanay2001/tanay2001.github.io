Tanay Dixit

Hi, I'm Tanay - a Research Scientist at Salesforce, where I work on training speech models.

I graduated with an MS in Computer Science from the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign, where I worked with Prof. Jiawei Han . I am honoured to be a Siebel Scholar (Class of 2025) . I graduated from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras in 2023 with a bachelor's degree in ECE, where I worked with Prof. Mitesh Khapra . I have first-author papers at ACL, EMNLP, and WWW on multi-agent systems and evaluation frameworks (1200+ citations).
